## Primary real-world reference
Hershesons — Look Book
https://www.hershesons.com/pages/look-book

The live page was inspected directly before implementation.

## Observed design logic
- the page begins almost immediately with the work rather than a conventional marketing hero;
- staggered / masonry-style portrait grid;
- white or very light background;
- portrait-oriented hair imagery;
- full-bleed images inside their slots;
- moderate white gaps between images;
- no text overlays;
- no captions;
- no numbering on individual looks;
- the photography carries the visual hierarchy;
- restrained navigation and almost no decorative UI;
- fashion-editorial rhythm rather than a salon before/after gallery.

## What SEZIONE adapts
- image-first editorial rhythm;
- staggered portrait grid;
- white field and moderate gaps;
- no per-image captions or fake client stories;
- portrait crops with varied scale;
- minimal interface around the images.

## What SEZIONE does not copy
- Hershesons photography;
- Hershesons logo or copy;
- exact grid coordinates;
- ecommerce/navigation elements;
- exact typography;
- exact number of looks.

## SEZIONE framing
The page adds a restrained opening statement and a short decision-principles section so the gallery does not imply that reference images are guaranteed client outcomes.

Core line:
“RIFERIMENTI. NON COPIE.”
