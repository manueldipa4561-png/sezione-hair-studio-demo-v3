# PRIMA VISITA — Reference Card

## Status
ACTIVE — Page 05

## Primary real-world reference
Josh Wood Colour — Consultation
https://joshwoodcolour.com/pages/consultation

The live page was inspected directly before implementation.

## Observed design / decision logic
- definitively text-first;
- minimal main-content imagery;
- one clear value proposition at the top;
- two equal consultation pathways presented side by side;
- each pathway has a short explanation and one action;
- strong whitespace and simple hierarchy;
- colored fields and typography provide visual distinction instead of photography;
- the user is guided into a decision without needing technical expertise first;
- on mobile, consultation choices collapse into a simpler vertical flow.

## What SEZIONE adapts
- text-first consultation hub;
- two-path decision gateway;
- short explanatory copy before technical service selection;
- generous whitespace;
- no decorative imagery just to fill space;
- clear route from uncertainty to an informed service family.

## What SEZIONE does not copy
- Josh Wood Colour name, logo or wording;
- its purple/yellow brand palette;
- product recommendation logic;
- automated quiz mechanics;
- video-booking mechanics;
- free-consultation claim;
- appointment length;
- staff claims;
- e-commerce or newsletter elements.

## SEZIONE two-path translation
Instead of “automated vs human consultation,” the demo uses:
- A — I have a reference.
- B — I only have an idea.

## Page information architecture
1. First-visit statement
2. Two starting pathways
3. What to bring
4. Four factors before choosing
5. Direction → reading → service sequence
6. Final route to Services / Lookbook

## Image plan
No imagery is used on Page 05.

Reason:
- the primary reference is text-first;
- photography would not solve a genuine content or conversion problem here;
- avoiding unnecessary imagery keeps the page faster and more focused.

## Content integrity
This is a concept demo.

Do not claim:
- booking availability;
- consultation duration;
- consultation price;
- specific pre-appointment requirements;
- staff assignment;
- medical or technical guarantees;
- guaranteed suitability of any service.

No personal data is collected and no booking form is implemented.
