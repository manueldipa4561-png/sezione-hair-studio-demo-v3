## Primary visual / structural reference
Josh Wood Colour Atelier — Services & Price List
https://joshwoodcolour.com/pages/hair-salon-price-list

### Observed live-page characteristics
- text-first hero rather than an image-heavy banner;
- predominantly white / neutral palette with near-black typography;
- bold sans-serif headings and clean supporting copy;
- service families exposed through a horizontal category filter;
- services are separated by whitespace rather than card shadows;
- descriptions explain what the service does;
- booking CTA is placed contextually next to service browsing;
- generous vertical spacing keeps a long menu readable.

### What SEZIONE adapts
- text-first Services hero;
- one-line family navigator directly under the hero;
- service families presented as open editorial sections, not boxed cards;
- each family explains outcome and decision criteria rather than only naming techniques;
- contextual “not sure what to choose?” route before deeper service browsing;
- restrained monochrome / natural-material palette inherited from the locked Home.

### What SEZIONE does NOT copy
- Josh Wood brand name, logo or copy;
- prices;
- staff tiers;
- proprietary service names;
- exact category set;
- exact gradient accent;
- exact page measurements;
- salon claims.

## Real service-selection friction identified
A new visitor needs to answer:
1. What family of service am I actually looking for?
2. What if I know the result but not the technical service name?
3. Do I need to start with a consultation / first visit?
4. What factors change the recommendation?
5. What is the next safe action if I am unsure?
